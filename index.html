Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — call us at (904) 606-5313 and we'll take a look at your roof and give you a clear, no-obligation estimate.

Do you do repairs, or only full replacements?

Both. Many roofs just need a targeted repair. If a full replacement would save you money in the long run, we'll tell you why — no pressure.

How do I know if I need a repair or a new roof?

Missing shingles, water stains on the ceiling, and granules in the gutters are warning signs. We'll inspect the roof and explain exactly what we find.

Can you help after a storm?

Yes. Florida storms lift shingles and open up leaks. We inspect storm damage, document what we find, and get the roof sealed and repaired.
